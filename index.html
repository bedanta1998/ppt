Structuring assignments and grading pipelines for online certification

Types of Assignment Questions Auto-Graded Formats

NPTEL supports a wide variety of auto-graded question formats configuration on the portal:

  • MCQ / MSQ: Multiple choice / select options.
  • Numerical: Fill-in-the-blanks or objective.
  • Alphanumeric: Text match patterns.
  • Numeric Array: For vectors & matrices.
  • Programming: Compiler-graded scripts.
[MCQ] Which of these are...
[NUM] Calculate value: [ ____ ]
[PROG] Python: def solution()...
Auto-Graded: MCQ & MSQ Details Multiple Choice / Select Options
  • MCQ Choice Density:
    • Provide up to 8 choices to reduce guessing probability (4 choices = 25% guess chance, 6 choices = 17% chance, 8 choices = 12.5% chance).
  • MSQ Option Guidelines:
    • Avoid using filler options like "None of the above" / "All of the above" in MSQs to prevent logical contradictions.
    • Strict Penalties: Selecting any incorrect option forfeits all marks (score = 0). Partial grading applies only if no incorrect choices are checked.
  • Descriptive Courses: Include a higher ratio of MSQs than MCQs to evaluate comprehension.
💡 Validation Tip: TAs must cross-check and independently solve each other's keys before assignments go live!
Auto-Graded: Numerical Questions Fill in the Blanks Rules
  • Numerical Specifications:
    • Answers accept only digits 0-9 and a single decimal point. Letters or units must not be typed by students (specify units in the question text).
    • Range Configuration: Unless the answer is an integer, always configure a range instead of an exact value (e.g., target 1.72 with ±0.01 tolerance -> 1.71 to 1.73).
    • Precision Exception: For 1-step problems, the range does not have to be broadened if you want students to learn the importance of not rounding off excessively.
⚠️ Important: Ensure configured ranges are wide enough to account for all reasonable approximations. Narrow ranges trigger heavy student complaints.
Alphanumeric, Array & Programming Advanced Question Configuration

Alphanumeric

Matches text values. Strictly uses Exact Match (fragile: any extra spaces, characters, or casing difference results in 0 marks). Use single-word answers.

⚠️ In exams, ONLY Exact Match is supported.

Numeric Array

Used to configure vectors, matrices, or array responses (e.g. checking all elements in order).

Programming

Code compilation checks. Supported languages: C, C++, Java, Python, Verilog, Perl, Haskell.

Manual Grading Assignments Subjective Submissions Guidelines

Submission & Staff Rules

  • Submission Options: Document Upload (PDF/Word) or Essay-type Text Area.
  • Add Course Staff: Must add all subjective grading staff to the portal before the assignment is made public to ensure correct submission allocation.

Evaluation & Folder Rules

  • Grading Scale: Evaluation must be done out of 100 total marks.
  • Grading Deadline: Strictly 2 weeks from assignment due date.
  • Folder Integrity Warning: Never delete or move files from the designated portal folder; it breaks access and makes documents inaccessible.

Best Practices

Pedagogical recommendations for high-impact course delivery

Best Practices: Videos & Interaction Video Standards & Student Support

Video & Summary Specs

Duration: 2.0 to 2.5 hours (120-150 mins) of recorded video lectures per week.

Video Summaries: Create 3-4 bullet summaries for each video (displayed beside the player).

Self-Evaluation: Create 2-3 self-test questions for every 10 mins of video.

Interaction & Office Hours

Office Hours: Hold 1 hour/week live video session (with instructor's consent) to clear closed/upcoming assignment doubts.

Study Material: Ensure slides/notes and additional reading references are uploaded weekly.

Discussion Forum

Engagement: Introduce yourself on the forum during the first week.

Moderation: Check the forum twice daily; resolve queries within 24 hours.

Proactive: Propose pedagogical questions to initiate discussions if activity is low.

Best Practices: Weekly Assignments Rigorous Quality Assurance & Verification Mode
Mandatory Pre-Release Verification Checklist

Before making any weekly assignment public, TAs must: (i) Keep "Check Answers" option ON and input keys to verify score is 100% correct, (ii) Try incorrect key combinations to test autograding rules, (iii) Turn the "Check Answers" option OFF before publishing, (iv) Ensure Multiple Submissions is enabled, and no question has its weight set to 0.

Release Checklist Summary

  • Cross-Checking: TAs must independently solve and verify each other's keys to prevent bugs.
  • Solutions: Releasing detailed step-by-step solved keys (or video solutions) is mandatory. Just the answers are not enough.

Ensure assignments are 100% error-free. Errors destroy candidate confidence in the final exam, where papers and keys are not released.

Descriptive Courses (Part 1) Auto-Grading Subjective Topics

Descriptive courses do not align naturally with auto-graded online systems, but key methods can bridge the gap:

  • Use a greater ratio of Multiple Select Questions (MSQs) than MCQs.
  • Add more choices in MCQs (ensure they are plausible distractors).
  • Comprehension and Grouped Questions: Very powerful. Provide a text passage/essay followed by a set of linked objective questions.
  • Identify elements of an image or use **Match-the-columns** options.
📧 Suggestion requests for question design? Email: nkaisare@nptel.iitm.ac.in. (Note: portal modifications require a 6-month lead time).
Descriptive Courses (Part 2) Managing Essay-Type Questions

Essay-Type Final Exams

We often include a few descriptive essay-type questions in the final exams. Avoid surprising the students to ensure they have a fair chance of success:

  • Announce in advance: Explicitly inform students that the final exam contains subjective questions.
  • Practice assignments: Configure essay-type questions in assignments to let them practice the submission portal.
  • Latter Half Focus: Introduce essay questions in the later weeks of the course.
  • Share sample keys: Provide sample answer documents to set expectations of grading criteria.

Additional Tips for Student Success Portal Enhancements
Align Assignment Style with Exam Style

Students fail to perform when assignments are entirely MCQ but the final proctored exam is descriptive/written.

Upcoming Portal Features

  • Parameterized Questions: Dynamic variable support in math questions. Each student gets unique numerical values, preventing copying of final answers.
  • We welcome portal feature proposals to support your pedagogical objectives.
Exam Setting Exercise Scale & deadliness
  • Create 2 sets of papers: Deliver both sets on schedule.
  • The Scale: Out of 1054 courses:
    • 374 courses are in Repeat mode.
    • 680 courses need brand-new papers (total of 1360 exam papers!).
  • A core team of 10 configures these papers on the TCS authoring platform. Respond to clarifications immediately.

Quality Concerns to Verify

  • Check correctness, formatting, and verify missing details.
  • Never repeat questions between the two sets.
  • Check options text, diagram clarity, and avoid low-count question sheets.
Exodus at 90 Minutes Student Exam Behavior

70% Students Leave Early

During the proctored exam, 70% of learners exit the exam hall exactly 90 minutes after the start.

  • Papers with only 20-30 MCQs are completed very quickly.
  • Recommendation: If the exam lacks complex numerical or comprehension problems, increase the size to 100 questions to test comprehension thoroughly.
  • Can include slightly modified repeats from weekly assignments.
